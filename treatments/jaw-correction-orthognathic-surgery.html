Jaw Correction / Orthognathic Surgery

Corrective surgery for significant jaw misalignment, improving bite function and facial balance together.

Call Now WhatsApp Us
About the Procedure

What it involves

When a bite mismatch is too significant for braces alone to correct, orthognathic surgery repositions the jaw itself. It's typically planned in coordination with orthodontic treatment, addressing both how the teeth meet and how the jaw and face align — often relevant after facial trauma or growth-related mismatches, and planned in detail before any surgical step is taken.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Addresses bite issues braces alone cannot fully correct
  • Improves facial symmetry and jaw function together
  • Detailed pre-surgical planning with orthodontic coordination
  • Surgical expertise in complex jaw repositioning
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. Assessment & ImagingBite, jaw position and facial structure are thoroughly evaluated.
  2. Coordinated PlanningSurgery is planned in coordination with any ongoing orthodontic treatment.
  3. Surgical CorrectionThe jaw is repositioned under general anaesthesia for proper alignment.
  4. Recovery & Follow-UpHealing and bite stability are monitored over the following months.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • Significant bite mismatch not correctable by braces alone
  • Jaw asymmetry affecting function or appearance
  • Difficulty chewing or closing the jaw properly
  • Orthodontic treatment recommending surgical correction
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

Recovery is more involved than routine dental procedures, typically spanning several weeks for initial healing with continued monitoring over following months as the bite stabilises.
