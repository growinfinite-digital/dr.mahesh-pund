Oral Biopsy & Lesion Removal

A precise diagnostic step for unexplained oral lesions, giving clear answers before deciding on further treatment.

Call Now WhatsApp Us
About the Procedure

What it involves

An unusual white or red patch, a persistent ulcer, or an unexplained growth in the mouth needs proper diagnosis, not guesswork. A biopsy takes a small tissue sample for laboratory analysis, and where a lesion needs to be removed entirely, that's carried out with the same surgical precision as any other oral procedure.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Provides a definitive diagnosis rather than a guess
  • Enables early identification of serious conditions
  • Minimally invasive sampling procedure
  • Guides the correct next treatment step clearly
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. ExaminationThe lesion or area of concern is carefully examined.
  2. Local AnaesthesiaThe area is numbed to keep the procedure comfortable.
  3. Biopsy or RemovalA tissue sample is taken, or the lesion is removed, depending on the case.
  4. Results & GuidanceResults are reviewed with you, along with recommended next steps.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • A mouth sore or ulcer that hasn't healed in two weeks
  • An unusual white or red patch in the mouth
  • An unexplained lump or growth in the oral cavity
  • A referral following a routine oral examination
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

The site typically heals within a week or so, with mild soreness managed easily. Results are usually discussed at a scheduled follow-up appointment.
