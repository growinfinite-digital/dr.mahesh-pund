Salivary Gland Disorders

Diagnosis and treatment for salivary gland swelling, blockages, or infections affecting the mouth and jaw area.

Call Now WhatsApp Us
About the Procedure

What it involves

Salivary glands can become blocked, infected or swollen — sometimes due to a stone forming within the gland, sometimes from infection. These conditions can cause pain, swelling near the jaw or under the tongue, and discomfort while eating, and are assessed and managed as part of the broader maxillofacial scope of practice.

Benefits

Why patients choose this treatment

  • Accurate diagnosis of the specific gland issue
  • Both surgical and non-surgical options considered
  • Relieves pain and swelling at the source
  • Prevents recurring infection where a blockage is the cause
Procedure

What to expect, step by step

  1. AssessmentThe affected gland and surrounding area are examined, often with imaging.
  2. DiagnosisThe specific cause — stone, infection or swelling — is identified.
  3. TreatmentManagement ranges from conservative care to surgical removal of a blockage.
  4. Follow-UpRecovery is monitored to confirm the issue has resolved.
Who Needs It

Signs this treatment may help you

  • Recurring swelling near the jaw or under the tongue
  • Pain while eating linked to a specific gland
  • Suspected salivary stone or blockage
  • A gland infection not resolving on its own
Recovery

Healing & aftercare

Recovery depends on whether treatment is conservative or surgical; most patients see steady improvement within days to a couple of weeks.
