Case 02 · Facial Trauma Surgery

Jaw fracture fixation — restoring bite and facial alignment

The case

The patient presented with facial trauma and was diagnosed with a left-sided Le Fort II fracture along with a left parasymphysis fracture.

The treatment

Open reduction and intermaxillary fixation were performed. Lateral brow and circumvestibular incisions were used to access the fracture sites, followed by fixation using miniplates and miniscrews.

The outcome

The fractures were stabilized with 2 mm miniplates and miniscrews, restoring proper alignment and providing stable fixation for healing.

Case 03 · Dental Implant

Implant with bone grafting — rebuilding bone for a lasting result

The case

Patient had missing teeth following reconstruction with a free fibular graft after carcinoma surgery 7 years ago. CBCT was used to assess the grafted bone and plan implant placement.

The treatment

Three implants were placed in the reconstructed fibular graft: two 4.5 × 8.5 mm implants in regions 34 and 45, and one 4 × 10 mm implant in the indicated region.

The outcome

The implants were allowed to integrate with the grafted bone before final restoration, providing a stable foundation for replacement teeth with improved chewing function and appearance.
